# 用量喵图标

设计：白色猫咪与三根用量柱状条组成一个标志，采用软件已有的深蓝、青蓝与浅紫配色。圆角方形底板，外部透明，强调小尺寸下的轮廓识别。

生成方式：内置 image_gen 工具。PNG 为生成原图，ICO 仅做尺寸与格式导出，包含 16、24、32、48、64、128、256 像素七种尺寸。

## 最终提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: finished Windows desktop application icon, square 1024 x 1024, one icon only.
Primary request: Design an original icon for 用量喵 (Usage Meow), a friendly desktop app for monitoring AI coding token usage and cost. No lettering in the image.
Subject: a charming, confident white cat silhouette combined intelligently with a simple three-column usage bar chart. Rounded cat head with two clear pointed ears, two tiny dark eyes and a restrained little smile; its lower body resolves into three chunky rounded chart columns of different heights. One cohesive recognizable symbol, not a cat beside a separate chart.
Scene/backdrop: deep midnight-blue rounded-square app tile with softly rounded corners; truly transparent canvas outside the tile, not a checkerboard picture. Tile fills about 90 percent of canvas. Icon sits comfortably within the tile with consistent padding.
Color palette: follow the existing app's dark navy, electric sky-blue and soft periwinkle accents, high-contrast ivory white cat. Restrained luminous blue accent on the chart columns.
Style: premium friendly desktop utility branding, extremely clean geometric shapes, subtle soft enamel depth and gentle lighting, crisp silhouette and clean edge finishing. Sophisticated but approachable, not furry or realistic.
Composition: centered, straight front view, balanced silhouette, bold readable forms that remain recognizable at 32 px.
Constraints: no words, no numbers, no watermark, no surrounding presentation board, no device mockup, no multiple variants, no coins, no currency symbols, no extra decoration, no fine whisker lines, no heavy drop shadow outside the tile. Deliver the icon asset itself.
